# Store texts — English (en-US, en-GB)

Same rules as `store/listing.md`: hard character limits, no superlatives, no
invented numbers, no references to other games. `scripts/asc-sprachen.py`
reads the fenced blocks below and writes them to App Store Connect for
**en-US and en-GB**. The headings are the same as in the German file so one
parser serves both.

**Why English at all.** Until 07.10.2026 the listing existed only in German —
the app was invisible to every English search in every storefront, although
the game itself has been fully playable in English for a long time (it follows
the device language).

## Apple App Store

### Name (max. 30)
```
Stack & Siege – Castle Duel
```

### Untertitel (max. 30)
```
Build walls. Breach the fort.
```

### Werbetext (max. 170)
```
Build your castle from falling blocks and blast your rival's walls. One gap in your wall decides the match. Online, with friends, or offline vs the bot.
```

### Beschreibung (max. 4000)
```
Two castles, a river in between. You build your wall from falling blocks — your opponent shoots it down again.

ONE RULE DECIDES
If your castle is not fully walled in when the build phase ends, you lose. Diagonals count. A single gap is enough, and your opponent knows exactly where it is.

THE RHYTHM
Build, shoot, arm up — and again. Every phase lasts seconds, every decision carries into the endgame.

TWO KINDS OF CANNON
The Wall Breaker tears holes into the defence. The Slayer only hits cannons and takes out your opponent's firepower — it is so expensive that in the first round you have to give up everything else to get it.

ONLINE OR ON YOUR OWN
Play online against random opponents, with friends via a code, two or three players on one device — or offline against the bot on three levels.

COLLECT AND FORGE
Spoils become upgrades, gold becomes cosmetics. Rare materials from online matches can be forged into pieces you cannot get in the shop.

HONESTLY
Stack & Siege is free to play. No ads, no pay-to-win — the full game is open without paying. Gold and cosmetics are a reward for playing.
```

### Schlüsselwörter (max. 100)
```
fortress,cannon,strategy,tactics,medieval,multiplayer,2,player,local,offline,bot,blocks,defense,pvp
```
Not repeated: words from name and subtitle (stack, siege, castle, duel, build,
walls, breach, fort) — Apple searches those anyway. No trademarks.
